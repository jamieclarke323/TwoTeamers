How to play

Name the footballers who have played for both of the (men's) teams listed. You can request clues for individual answers, or for all.

You can also give up on one answer from its card. It will show as ❌ when sharing; you can keep scoring for the other players.

If you're unsure of the spelling, click 'Guess' and close attempts will be treated as correct answers.

Each quiz will only award correct answers for players who played for both clubs from the 1999/2000 season onwards. If a player played for one club before 99/00 and one club after, they will not be counted.

When sharing the game with others, you will be awarded a:

🟢 for a correct answer found using no clues

🟡 where you've used one clue

🟠 where you've used two or more clues

❌ indicates an answer not found
