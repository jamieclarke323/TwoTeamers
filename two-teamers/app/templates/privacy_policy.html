Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to Two Teamers!

Two Teamers is a football quiz game where you try to name players who have played for different combinations of football clubs.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information needed to operate the game, save your scores and personalise your experience.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Two Teamers is an independently operated football quiz website based in the United Kingdom.

For questions about your privacy or personal information, please read the below, or contact twoteamerssupport@gmail.com

2. What information we collect

You can play Two Teamers with or without an account.

Playing without an account

When you play as a guest, we use a cookie containing an anonymous visitor ID to recognise your browser.

This ID allows us to associate your game results with your browser so that you can return to your recent scores.

The cookie does not contain your name, email address or scores. Your scores are stored separately in our database and linked to your visitor ID.

The cookie has a 30-day lifetime, which is renewed when you use certain quiz or fixture pages.

If the cookie expires, is deleted or becomes unavailable, we may no longer be able to retrieve your previous guest results. The same applies if you switch browsers or devices, or use private browsing.

The expiry of the cookie does not automatically delete the scores associated with it.

Creating an account

If you register, we collect:

  • Email address: To identify your account and enable you to log in.
  • Password information: To authenticate your login.
  • Favourite football team: To personalise certain areas of gameplay.
  • Game scores and history: To allow you to access your results over time.

These details are private and are not displayed to other players.

Your login session normally lasts up to 30 days. If your session expires or you log out, your scores remain saved against your account and become accessible again when you log back in.

Transferring guest scores to an account

If you register or log in using the same browser while your guest identification cookie is still available, your saved guest results are transferred to your account.

If your account already contains a result for the same quiz, the existing account result takes priority.

This allows you to keep your guest results when moving to a registered account.

Technical information

Our website and hosting provider may also process technical information, including IP addresses, browser information, request times and server logs.

This information is used to deliver the website, maintain security and investigate technical problems.

3. How we use your information

We use information to:

  • Operate the football quiz and its features.
  • Record and retrieve game results.
  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and maintain login sessions.
  • Personalise gameplay using favourite football teams.
  • Transfer guest scores to registered accounts.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the website.
  • Respond to enquiries and privacy requests.
  • Sending optional gameplay-related push notifications

We do not currently use third-party analytics tools, send marketing emails or serve targeted advertisements.

4. Our legal basis for processing information

Under UK data protection law, we must have a lawful basis for processing personal information.

We generally rely on:

Performance of a contract: To provide registered users with account access, saved results and personalised game features.

Legitimate interests: To operate and secure the website, maintain essential technical records and provide temporary guest gameplay features.

Consent

Where you choose to enable optional push notifications, we rely on your consent to process the information needed to send them.

These notifications remind you when a Two Teamers game is available to play that day.

You can disable notifications at any time through your device or browser settings, or through your account preferences where available.

Where consent is required for cookies or other processing activities, we will obtain it separately.

5. How long we keep your information

Different information is retained for different periods.

Guest identification cookies

The guest identification cookie has a lifetime of 30 days.

This period is renewed when you visit quiz or fixture pages that establish your guest identity.

If you stop using those pages, the cookie will normally expire after 30 days.

Deleting the cookie, changing browsers or using a different device can also prevent us from reconnecting you with earlier guest results.

Guest game results

Guest results are stored in our database separately from the browser cookie.

These records may remain in the database after the cookie expires or is deleted, even where they are no longer accessible to the original visitor.

We will review our retention practices and delete or anonymise personal information when it is no longer needed for a legitimate purpose.

Registered accounts

Account information, favourite team selections and game results are retained while an account continues to exist.

Under the current system, game scores do not automatically expire after a specified period.

Logging out or allowing a login session to expire does not delete account information or scores.

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting us.

Technical information

Technical and security records may be retained for limited periods by our hosting provider for operational and security purposes.

6. Cookies and browser storage

Two Teamers uses cookies to support gameplay and account functionality.

These include:

Guest identification cookie

Stores an anonymous visitor ID that allows us to associate recent quiz scores with your browser. The cookie lasts 30 days and may be renewed through continued use of relevant game pages.

Login and session cookies

Allow registered players to access their accounts securely and remain logged in. Login sessions normally have a 30-day lifetime.

How to play preference

We store the time of your most recent page visit in your browser's local storage. This prevents the How to play instructions from opening automatically again unless you are logged out and have not visited for more than seven days. The timestamp is updated on each page visit, stays in your browser and can be removed by clearing site data. You can always open the instructions using the How to play button.

We do not currently use third-party analytics or advertising cookies.

We use cookies to provide game and account features. We will assess the applicable legal consent requirements and obtain consent where necessary.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. However, deleting cookies may log you out or prevent access to previously saved guest results.

7. Where your information is stored and who we share it with

Two Teamers is hosted using PythonAnywhere, a third-party web hosting platform.

Our hosting infrastructure processes the information needed to operate the website, including account information, game records and relevant technical data.

We do not sell your personal information.

Your email address, favourite football team and individual game results are not publicly shared with other players.

Information may be processed by our hosting provider where necessary to deliver the website or maintain its infrastructure.

We may also disclose information where legally required or where necessary to protect website security.

Where personal information is transferred outside the United Kingdom, we will comply with applicable international data transfer requirements.

8. Advertising

Two Teamers does not currently display third-party advertisements.

9. Children's privacy

Two Teamers is a football quiz intended for a general audience, including younger football fans.

Players can enjoy the game without registering an account or providing an email address.

We aim to minimise the personal information collected and do not make registered users' account details or individual scores publicly available.

We do not currently use personal information for targeted advertising or third-party analytics.

We recognise that children's personal information requires particular protection and aim to provide appropriate privacy safeguards.

Parents, guardians and younger players can contact us with questions about personal information.

10. Keeping your information secure

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the personal information we hold.

These measures include appropriate safeguards for user authentication, database access and website hosting.

No internet-based service can guarantee complete security, but we aim to reduce the risk of unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

11. Push notifications

Two Teamers offers optional push notifications to users who have installed the website as a web app on a supported device.

If you choose to enable notifications, we may collect and store technical information needed to deliver them, including a unique push subscription identifier, notification delivery endpoint and associated encryption keys.

This information is used solely to send notifications relating to Two Teamers and its gameplay features.

Push notifications are entirely optional. We will only enable them after you have chosen to opt in and granted notification permission through your device or browser.

Notifications are delivered using the push notification services provided by your browser or operating system. These services may process the technical information necessary to deliver notifications to your device.

You can disable notifications at any time through your device or browser settings. Where available, you can also manage your notification preferences through your Two Teamers account.

We retain push subscription information while it is needed to provide notifications and remove it when it is no longer required, including where we identify that a subscription is no longer valid.

We do not use push notification subscriptions for advertising or unrelated marketing purposes.

12. Your privacy rights

Under UK data protection law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Request restriction of certain processing activities.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Request a portable copy of certain information, where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on your consent.

These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact twoteamerssupport@gmail.com

Account deletion

Two Teamers does not currently provide an automated account deletion feature.

You can request deletion of your account and its associated personal information by contacting us.

We will handle your request in accordance with applicable data protection law, subject to any lawful reasons for retaining particular records.

Guest data requests

Because guest results are associated with an anonymous browser identifier rather than an email address, we may need information that allows us to locate the relevant records before processing a request.

If the identification cookie has been lost or expired, we may be unable to establish which guest records belong to you.
